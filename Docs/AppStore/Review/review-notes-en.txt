ParentalControl helps parents manage a child's app usage from their own phone. It requires two iPhones running iOS 18.4 or later and an internet connection. No registration or demo login is required.

SETUP
Select the parent role on one phone and the child role on the other, then follow onboarding to pair them using the displayed code.
On the child phone, authorize Screen Time and select apps to manage. Allow notifications on both phones. Background location requests require Always location permission on the child phone.
Set a parental PIN during setup or in the parent phone's Settings. This PIN opens Parent mode on the child phone, including detailed usage statistics.

IMPORTANT DETAILS
Task photo reports use the iOS share sheet. After the child takes or selects a photo and completes sharing to the parent, the task is marked as submitted for approval. Photos are not uploaded to our server; there is no built-in chat or public feed.
Removal protection is optional and requires Apple Family Sharing with child authorization. It is not needed for basic setup.

PREMIUM
The app includes 24 hours of full access after paired-device setup. Continued parental control requires a monthly or annual subscription, or a lifetime purchase. The 24-hour demo is separate from the annual subscription's 3-day introductory trial for eligible Apple accounts.
Settings > Premium opens the paywall at any time, including during the demo. Restore is available on the paywall.
